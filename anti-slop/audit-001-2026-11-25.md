## Daftar Temuan Bernomor

### A. `/antislop-human` (Aksesibilitas, Kontras WCAG, Keyboard, Interaksi)

1. **[HIGH | R-39] Viewport memblokir pinch-to-zoom di perangkat mobile.**
   - **Lokasi:** [index.html](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/index.html#L5)
   - **Detail:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=no" />` mencegah pengguna memperbesar layar.
   - **Perbaikan:** Ubah menjadi `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`.

2. **[HIGH | R-25] Kontras teks gagal WCAG AA pada tombol CTA gradien gelap, Hero Card tanpa scrim, dan teks `slate-500`.**
   - **Lokasi:** [HubHomePage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubHomePage.tsx#L130-L134), [HubVariantSetupPage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubVariantSetupPage.tsx#L201-L216), [HubHowToPlayPage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubHowToPlayPage.tsx#L37), [HubSettingsPage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubSettingsPage.tsx#L115)
   - **Bukti Terukur (WCAG 2.x via Node luminance):**
     - `text-slate-950` di atas gradien No Mercy (`#dc2626` -> `#be123c` -> `#0f172a`): **4.18:1**, **3.21:1**, dan **1.13:1** (FAIL ALL, teks hampir tidak terbaca di ujung kanan).
     - `text-slate-950` di atas Flip (`#9333ea`): **3.75:1** (FAIL AA normal) dan Liar's (`#7c3aed`): **3.54:1** (FAIL AA normal).
     - `text-white` di atas Hero Card `HubVariantSetupPage` tanpa overlay gelap: `amber-400` (**1.67:1**), `yellow-400` (**1.53:1**), `lime-400` (**1.51:1**), `cyan-400` (**1.81:1**) (FAIL ALL).
     - `text-slate-500` (`#64748b`) di atas `slate-900` (`#0f172a`): **3.75:1** (FAIL AA normal, dipakai pada placeholder input dan label `Kosong`).
   - **Perbaikan:** Tambahkan overlay scrim gelap pada Hero Card, gunakan warna teks tombol yang adaptif/kontras tinggi (`>= 4.5:1`) atau warna tombol solid terkontrol per mode, dan naikkan `text-slate-500` menjadi minimal `text-slate-400` (`6.96:1`).

3. **[HIGH | R-32] `focus:outline-none` menghapus indikator fokus keyboard tanpa pengganti `:focus-visible`.**
   - **Lokasi:** [HubHomePage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubHomePage.tsx#L214), [HubSettingsPage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubSettingsPage.tsx#L85), [HubOnlineLobbyPage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubOnlineLobbyPage.tsx#L263), dan input nama pemain di `HubVariantSetupPage.tsx`.
   - **Perbaikan:** Hapus `focus:outline-none` tanpa ring; terapkan style global `:focus-visible` dengan ring kontras tinggi (`amber-400`, offset `2px`) di `src/index.css`.

4. **[HIGH | R-32, R-39] Tombol khusus ikon tidak memiliki `aria-label`, modal tidak punya `role="dialog"` / Escape handler, dan `<label>` tidak terhubung `htmlFor`.**
   - **Lokasi:** [HubHomePage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubHomePage.tsx#L144-L151), [HubVariantSetupPage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubVariantSetupPage.tsx#L237-L246), [HubSettingsPage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubSettingsPage.tsx#L74-L86), [HubGamePage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubGamePage.tsx#L483-L520) & `#L1261-L1500`.
   - **Perbaikan:** Tambahkan `aria-label` bahasa Indonesia pada semua icon-only button, hubungkan `<label htmlFor>` ke `id` input, tambahkan `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"` dan handler tombol `Escape` pada modal yang bisa ditutup.

5. **[HIGH | R-39] Tidak ada dukungan `prefers-reduced-motion` dan aturan base `cursor: pointer`, sementara animasi `animate-pulse` / `animate-bounce` berjalan terus-menerus (> 5 detik).**
   - **Lokasi:** [src/index.css](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/index.css#L1-L15), [HubGamePage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubGamePage.tsx), [HubOnlineLobbyPage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/hub/pages/HubOnlineLobbyPage.tsx#L172), [GamePage.tsx](file:///c:/Dev-Kode%20Pemrograman/All%20Project/uno-zero/src/pages/GamePage.tsx#L513).
   - **Perbaikan:** Tambahkan `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` dan base-layer `button:not(:disabled) { cursor: pointer; }` di `src/index.css`, serta hilangkan loop `animate-bounce` / `animate-pulse` tanpa batas pada tombol aksi.
